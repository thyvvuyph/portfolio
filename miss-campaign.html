MISS

MISS is a romantic psychological music video exploring grief, memory, and illusion. After losing his lover in a car accident, a man becomes trapped between reality and hallucinations of her presence.

Inspired by classic Hong Kong cinema, the visual direction combines cool greens with warm reds and browns to create a cinematic mood that feels intimate, nostalgic, and melancholic.
