Being Bees Honey

The concept was developed around Being Bees' connection to nature and the idea of bringing the journey from hive to table into the digital experience. Rather than treating honey as simply a sweet product, the direction focuses on the natural process behind it — from the bees and flowers to the raw honey that eventually becomes part of everyday rituals.

UI/UX DEV Shopify Liquid Email Design

The design direction was guided by the brand's connection to bees, nature, and raw honey, while keeping the existing visual elements recognisable and consistent. I explored how typography, imagery, layout, and graphic elements could work together to communicate the brand story while creating a clear and intuitive experience for customers.

A key consideration was finding the balance between the organic character of the brand and the functional needs of an e-commerce website. Product information, storytelling, and navigation were structured to feel approachable and easy to explore, while visual details were used to bring more personality and warmth to the experience.

The final concept builds upon the brand's identity — creating a digital presence that feels authentic to Being Bees Honey while giving the brand a more cohesive and engaging way to connect with its audience.
