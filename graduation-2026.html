Graduation 2026

Key visual and event collateral for Hoang Hoa Tham Secondary School's Graduation 2026 ceremony. The concept was developed around the idea of celebrating an important milestone while capturing the excitement, pride, and sense of achievement that comes with graduation. The visual direction aims to create an identity that feels youthful and celebratory, while still carrying the formality and significance of the occasion.

Event Key Visual Print Design

The design language was built around a bold and expressive visual system, using strong typography, layered graphic elements, and a vibrant red and gold palette to create a sense of energy and prestige. The composition was designed to feel dynamic and memorable, reflecting the excitement of students stepping into a new chapter.

Beyond the main key visual, the concept was extended across event touchpoints such as backstage ID cards and printed materials. Each element was designed to work as part of the same visual system while remaining functional in an event environment, creating a consistent experience from backstage to the main ceremony.

The final direction brings together celebration, youthful energy, and ceremonial elegance — creating a visual identity that reflects both the significance of the occasion and the spirit of the graduating class.
