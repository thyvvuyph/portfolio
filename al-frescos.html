The Al Fresco's Group

The project explored how social media content could capture the distinct personality of each restaurant brand while creating a strong and engaging visual presence across the group. The direction focused on combining food photography, typography, graphic elements, and promotional messaging to make each post feel visually appealing while communicating the offer clearly.

Social Media Graphic Design F&B

A key part of the process was adapting the visual approach to suit different audiences and dining experiences. From the casual and generous character of Al Fresco’s to the more relaxed sophistication of Jaspas and the refined atmosphere of Jacksons Steakhouse, each brand required its own balance of imagery, composition, and visual tone.

The content was designed with social media in mind, where visuals need to communicate quickly and create an immediate appetite appeal. Promotional offers, seasonal campaigns, and food-focused content were structured around clear visual hierarchy, allowing the key message and product to remain the focus.

Across the different brands, the work aimed to create content that feels distinctive to each restaurant while remaining cohesive as part of the wider group, demonstrating how a flexible visual approach can adapt to different brand personalities, campaigns, and audiences.
