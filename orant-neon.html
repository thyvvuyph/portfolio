Orant Neon

The creative direction focused on presenting Orant Neon’s products through dynamic visual content designed to capture attention across digital and promotional platforms. The series demonstrates how Orant Neon’s LED products can be presented across different themes and environments, from seasonal promotions to sports-inspired content, while maintaining a bold, dynamic, and recognisable visual presence.

Each video was structured around a clear visual rhythm, allowing the product and key message to stand out within a short viewing time. Different compositions and motion treatments were explored to maintain visual interest while keeping the LED displays as the focal point.
