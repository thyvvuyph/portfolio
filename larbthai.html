LarbThai

For LarbThai’s promotional campaign, the visual direction centres on creating an energetic and appetising impression that reflects the bold character of Thai cuisine. Food imagery, expressive typography, and graphic composition come together to create a visual that feels vibrant, inviting, and attention-grabbing.

Social Media Graphic Design F&B

Concept Development

The LED poster serves as the main visual, with a layout designed for immediate impact and readability from a distance. Large-scale typography, striking food imagery, and a clear hierarchy help communicate the key promotional message while drawing attention to the food.

The same visual language was then adapted into a flyer, with the composition restructured to work within a smaller format. Additional information could be introduced while maintaining the visual impact and recognisability of the original LED poster.

The final campaign creates a cohesive connection between digital and print, balancing bold communication, appetising imagery, and practical information to make the promotion engaging and easy to understand.
