Logo Design

A collection of logo designs created for multiple brands across different industries. Each logo is crafted to reflect the brand's identity, values, and target audience with clean, memorable marks.

Branding Identity

Neo

Neo is an AI assistant concept designed to make everyday interactions with AI feel more approachable and engaging. The logo combines the forms of a cat and a flame, using two familiar symbols to express the personality and role of the assistant.

The cat represents curiosity, intelligence, and companionship, giving Neo a friendly character and making the technology feel less distant or robotic. Its playful and recognisable form helps create an approachable personality that users can easily connect with. The flame represents the spark behind ideas — suggesting intelligence, creativity, energy, and the ability to turn a thought into something more. This connects with the role of an AI assistant as a tool for exploring ideas, solving problems, and supporting creative thinking.

Rather than representing AI through conventional technology symbols, the logo uses these two elements to create a more smart, curious, personable and memorable identity. The identity aims to position Neo as more than just a piece of technology — a smart, approachable assistant that users can naturally interact with.

Hopper

Hopper is a design studio and creative agency concept inspired by the idea of a hopping rabbit. The rabbit was chosen as a symbol of speed, agility, and quick thinking — qualities that reflect the fast-paced and constantly evolving nature of creative work. The logo captures the rabbit in a simplified and playful form, focusing on its distinctive movement and silhouette. The idea of “hopping” also represents the way creative ideas can move quickly from one direction to another, encouraging experimentation, spontaneity, and unexpected solutions.

The colour palette adds energy and personality to the identity, creating a bold and contemporary impression while helping the studio stand out in a competitive creative environment. The colours were selected to complement the playful character of the rabbit while maintaining a professional and versatile visual presence. The identity was designed to feel quick, curious, playful, and adaptable, reflecting a studio that is always ready to move, explore new ideas, and take on different creative challenges. The simple and recognisable form also allows the logo to work flexibly across digital platforms, studio applications, and creative projects.

Coconút

Coconút is a beauty and personal care brand centred around organic Coconut Oil and Vitamin E. The concept explores how the natural qualities of the ingredients could be expressed through a visual identity that feels gentle, approachable, and contemporary.

The development process began with exploring different visual possibilities and ways to represent the coconut and its natural benefits. Initial concepts experimented with more structured and formal shapes, gradually moving towards softer and more organic approaches as the brand direction became clearer. Through this exploration, the focus shifted towards a minimal, hand-drawn visual language. Instead of relying on formal geometric shapes, the final direction uses simple and friendly lines to create a more natural and human feeling. The imperfect quality of the drawing gives the identity a sense of warmth and authenticity, reflecting the organic nature of the products.

The final logo, positioned at the centre of the development, represents the outcome of this refinement process — reducing the visual elements to a simple and recognisable mark while maintaining the character of the brand. The design aims to communicate natural beauty, simplicity, and everyday self-care without feeling overly clinical or formal. Overall, the identity balances organic qualities with a clean and minimal approach, creating a friendly visual personality that can work naturally across cosmetic packaging, personal care products, and digital applications.

Lâm Coffee & Workshop

The concept is a coffee shop and creative workshop concept that combines the relaxed atmosphere of a coffee with a hands-on activity where customers can colour and personalise plaster statues. The identity was developed to capture both sides of the experience — a place to slow down over coffee while having the freedom to create something of your own.

The concept explores the idea of “coffee and creativity” through a friendly and playful visual language. Rather than following a conventional coffee identity, the logo incorporates a more expressive approach that reflects the fun and tactile nature of the workshop. The visual direction is designed to feel approachable and welcoming, appealing to customers who are looking for an experience rather than simply a place to have a drink. The plaster colouring activity became an important part of the concept, inspiring a visual language based on simple shapes, playful forms, and the act of making by hand. The identity aims to reflect the small moments of creativity that happen naturally in the space — choosing colours, painting a figure, sharing the experience with friends, and enjoying a cup of coffee along the way.
